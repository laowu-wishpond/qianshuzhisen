    森林公約Forest Covenant

    守護森林・守護美好Protect the forest, protect what's good

    入住時，我們會請您與同行旅人一起閱讀並簽署這份公約——也是您可以帶回家的紀念。 At check-in, we'll ask you and your fellow travelers to read and sign the covenant below — yours to take home as a keepsake.

    下載完整公約 PDF（可列印） Download Full Covenant PDF (printable)

    親愛的旅人，歡迎回到森林。這裡不只是露營區，更是一座陪伴這片土地走過數十年的森林。真正的放鬆不來自熱鬧，而來自安靜——誠摯邀請每位來到這裡的旅人，一起守護這片森林，也守護彼此難得的寧靜時光。入住前請詳閱以下公約，我們也會在入住時請您與同行旅人共同簽署確認。 Dear traveler, welcome back to the forest. This is more than a campground — it's a forest that has grown alongside this land for decades. True relaxation doesn't come from noise, but from quiet. We invite every guest to help protect this forest and the peaceful time we all share here. Please read the covenant below before your stay; we'll also ask you and your fellow travelers to sign it together at check-in.

    第一章　守護森林的寧靜Chapter 1 — Protecting the Forest's Tranquility

    森林最美的聲音，是風吹樹梢、水流潺潺、蟲鳴鳥叫。因此我們共同約定：
    ・不使用 KTV、卡拉OK、大型擴音器、高分貝音響等設備
    ・不舉辦影響他人休息之喧鬧聚會
    ・每日晚間 22:00 至翌日上午 7:00 為森林靜音時段，請降低談話及娛樂音量
    若經管家善意提醒仍持續影響其他旅人，本園區將終止住宿服務，並依住宿約定辦理。     The forest's most beautiful sounds are wind through the treetops, flowing water, and birdsong. So we ask everyone to agree to the following:
    ・No karaoke, large speakers, or high-decibel audio equipment
    ・No noisy gatherings that disturb other guests
    ・Quiet hours are 10 PM–7 AM daily — please keep conversation and entertainment volume low
    If a friendly reminder from our staff goes unheeded and the disturbance continues, we reserve the right to end your stay per the lodging agreement.

    第二章　守護森林的生命Chapter 2 — Protecting the Forest's Life

    森林，是千樹之森真正的主人。請與我們一起珍惜它：
    ・不攀折樹木、花草及植栽
    ・不任意挖掘地面或破壞草皮
    ・營釘請依指定位置及管家建議施作
    ・請勿餵食野生動物
    ・請共同維護自然生態，不留下任何人為破壞     The forest is the true owner of this land. Please help us cherish it:
    ・Do not pick or break trees, flowers, or plants
    ・Do not dig into the ground or damage the grass
    ・Please place tent stakes only in designated spots or as advised by our staff
    ・Please do not feed wild animals
    ・Help us protect the natural ecosystem — leave no trace

    第三章　守護共同的美好Chapter 3 — Protecting What We Share

    為讓每位旅人都能享受舒適的住宿品質，請共同維護：
    ・公共衛浴及公共空間
    ・垃圾分類及環境整潔
    ・愛惜公共設施
    ・尊重其他旅人的休息與隱私
    森林因彼此尊重，而更加美好。     So every guest can enjoy a comfortable stay, please help us maintain:
    ・Public restrooms and shared spaces
    ・Trash sorting and a clean environment
    ・Care for shared facilities
    ・Respect for other guests' rest and privacy
    The forest is better for everyone when we respect each other.

    第四章　安全與永續Chapter 4 — Safety & Sustainability

    為維護全區供電安全與公平使用，請勿使用高耗電設備，包括：移動式冷氣、電暖器、電磁爐、微波爐、大型烤箱。如因違規使用造成設備損壞，須負相關賠償責任。本園區依法合法經營，並投保公共意外責任保險，讓每位旅人安心享受森林假期。 To protect the site's power supply and ensure fair use, please do not bring high-power appliances, including portable air conditioners, space heaters, induction cooktops, microwaves, or large ovens. Guests are responsible for any equipment damage caused by violating this rule. We operate legally and carry public liability insurance, so you can enjoy your forest getaway with peace of mind.

    森林旅人承諾Forest Traveler's Pledge

    謝謝您願意放慢腳步，也謝謝您願意成為森林的一份子。願今天離開時，您帶走的是美好的回憶，留下的是依然純淨的森林。 Thank you for slowing down, and for becoming part of this forest. May you leave with beautiful memories — and leave the forest just as pure as you found it.

    本人已閱讀並了解《水松千樹之森 森林公約》，願意與同行旅人共同遵守上述約定，一起守護森林、尊重自然，也尊重每一位來到這裡的人。（入住時將於現場請您與同行旅人共同簽署本公約） I have read and understood the Shuisong Thousand-Tree Forest Covenant, and agree — together with my fellow travelers — to follow the above rules, protect the forest, respect nature, and respect everyone who comes here. (You'll be asked to sign this together with your group at check-in.)
