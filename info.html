    營區資訊Campground Info

    地址與海拔Address & elevation

    545 南投縣埔里鎮牛尾路 1-12 號後方
    海拔約 500～600 公尺。找不到營區時，導航「埔里地藏院」就對了。     Behind No. 1-12, Niuwei Rd., Puli Township, Nantou County 545
    Elevation approx. 500–600 m. If you can't find us, navigate to “Puli Dizang Temple”.

    營區規模About the grounds

    五公頃原始林地，最多可容納 56 帳。園區約有 2,600 棵平均樹齡 50 年的老樹環繞。 Five hectares of primal forest, up to 56 tents, surrounded by about 2,600 trees averaging 50 years old.

    營位與價格Sites & prices

    營位整塊出租，不限帳篷數量、也不依人數加價。每個營位價格不同：平日（週日至週四）約 NT$700～4,000，假日（週五、週六、國定假日）約 NT$900～6,000。各營位價格請見「預約日曆」頁。 Each site is rented as a whole plot, with no limit on tents and no per-person charge. Prices vary by site: weekdays (Sun–Thu) about NT$700–4,000, holidays (Fri, Sat, public holidays) about NT$900–6,000. See the Booking page for each site's price.

    設施Facilities

    自帶帳篷露營。園區提供公共衛浴（乾濕分離、定時清潔）、全區 24 小時不斷電供應、公共盥洗台與分類回收站，以及引瀑布活水的泡腳池。 Bring your own tent. We provide shared restrooms (separate wet and dry areas, cleaned regularly), 24-hour power across the grounds, shared wash basins, recycling stations, and a foot-soak pool fed by waterfall water.

    寵物Pets

    寵物友善，歡迎毛小孩一起來露營。愛護森林，毛小孩的排泄物請自行清理喔。 Pet-friendly: your furry friends are welcome. To keep the forest clean, please pick up after your pets.

    夜衝（前一晚入場）Early arrival (evening before)

    可加訂夜衝，前一晚 18:00～22:00 進場，價格為前一晚當天價格的 5 折（平日價的 5 折或假日價的 5 折）。過年期間（2027/2/4～2/10）不開放夜衝，且每晚價格加 3 成。 Add an early arrival to check in the evening before, 18:00–22:00, at 50% of that night's price (weekday or holiday rate). Not available during Lunar New Year (2027/2/4–2/10), when nightly prices are 30% higher.

    預約方式How to book

    線上選好日期與營位、填寫入住人數後送出，系統會為您保留營位 6 小時。請在 6 小時內匯款，並回報匯款帳號末五碼；逾時未回報，營位會自動釋出。我們查帳以後，會主動寄 email 通知您預約成功。 Choose your dates and site online, enter the number of guests and submit. We hold the site for 6 hours: please transfer payment within that time and report the last 5 digits of your account. If we don't hear from you in time, the site is released automatically.

    森林靜音時段Quiet hours

    每日晚間 22:00 至翌日上午 7:00 為森林靜音時段，請降低談話及娛樂音量。詳見「森林公約」。 Quiet hours are 22:00 to 7:00 every day. Please keep voices and music low. See the Forest Covenant for details.
