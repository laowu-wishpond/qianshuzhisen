    服務條款與退訂政策Terms & Cancellation Policy

    訂位流程Booking process

    於「預約日曆」頁面送出訂位申請後，我們會透過電話或簡訊與您確認日期、帳篷數與人數。訂位須於完成付款後才正式生效；未付款前，該日期的營位不予保留。 After submitting a request on the Booking page, we'll contact you by phone or text to confirm the date, number of tents, and headcount. A booking is only confirmed once payment is complete — the site is not held before payment.

    付款方式Payment methods

    我們提供銀行匯款與線上信用卡刷卡（綠界金流）兩種付款方式，詳見「聯絡我們」頁面之匯款資訊。線上刷卡由綠界科技代為處理，本站不會接觸或儲存您的完整卡號。 We accept bank transfer and online credit card payment (via ECPay). Card payments are processed by ECPay — this site never sees or stores your full card number.

    取消與退款規則Cancellation & refund policy

    因營位需提前備料整理，取消訂位將依入住日期遠近退款：
    ・入住日 7 天前取消：全額退款
    ・入住日 3–6 天前取消：退還 50%
    ・入住日前 48 小時內取消或未到場：恕不退款
    如需更改日期，請於入住日 3 天前與我們聯繫，將視營位狀況協助調整。     Because sites are prepared in advance, refunds follow this schedule:
    ・7+ days before arrival: full refund
    ・3–6 days before arrival: 50% refund
    ・Within 48 hours of arrival, or no-show: no refund
    To change your date, please contact us at least 3 days before arrival — we'll do our best to accommodate based on availability.

    天候與不可抗力Weather & force majeure

    如遇颱風、豪雨等主管機關發布停止上班上課或政府劃定之天災情事，導致營區暫停開放，將協助您全額退款或改期，不收取任何手續費。 If the campground must close due to a typhoon, severe weather, or other officially declared emergency, we'll offer a full refund or free rescheduling — no fees.

    聯絡我們Contact

    若對訂位、付款或退款規則有任何疑問，歡迎透過「聯絡我們」頁面或電話 0939-235-168 與我們聯繫。 Questions about booking, payment, or cancellations? Reach us via the Contact page or by phone at 0939-235-168.
