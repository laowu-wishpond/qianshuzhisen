交通指引Directions

如何抵達千樹之森How to get to Shuisong Thousand-Tree Forest

建議開車前往。國道6號下「愛蘭交流道」後，依 Google 地圖導航搜尋「千樹之森旗艦露營場」即可抵達；若導航不到，可先以「埔里地藏院」為地標，我們就在旁邊。山區路段會車請放慢車速。 Driving is recommended. Exit National Freeway No. 6 at the "Ailan Interchange," then follow Google Maps to "千樹之森旗艦露營場 (Shuisong Thousand-Tree Forest Flagship Campground)." If your map app can't find it, search "埔里地藏院 (Puli Dizang Temple)" instead — we're right next door. Please slow down on the mountain road when passing oncoming traffic.
