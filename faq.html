    常見問題FAQ

    需要自備帳篷嗎？Do I need to bring my own tent?

    是的，目前為自帶帳篷露營模式，我們提供營位、公設衛浴與不斷電供電服務。 Yes — this is a bring-your-own-tent campground. We provide the site, shared bathrooms, and uninterrupted power.

    費用怎麼計算？How is the price calculated?

    一帳（2人）NT$1,200 起，超過 2 人每加 1 人加收 NT$300。詳細金額可在「預約日曆」頁面即時試算。 From NT$1,200 per tent (2 people), +NT$300 per extra person. Get an instant estimate on the Booking page.

    如何確認預約成功？How do I know my booking is confirmed?

    送出線上預約申請後，我們會透過電話與您確認日期、人數與付款方式，完成付款後訂位才算確認。 After you submit a request, we'll call to confirm your date, headcount, and payment method. The booking is only final once payment is complete.

    營區有供電嗎？Is there power on site?

    全區採不斷電供應，帳篷、行動裝置都能安心充電使用。 Power runs across the whole site, so tents and devices can charge without worry.

    可以攜帶寵物嗎？Are pets allowed?

    細節規定將由營主確認後補充於此頁面。 Details to be confirmed by the campground owner and added here.

    如何前往？How do I get there?

    詳細交通資訊將於「聯絡我們」頁面提供，或直接與我們聯繫索取地圖。 Directions will be posted on the Contact page, or reach out to us directly for a map.
