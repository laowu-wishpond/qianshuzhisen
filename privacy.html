    隱私權政策Privacy Policy

    我們蒐集哪些資料What we collect

    當您透過「預約日曆」頁面送出訂位申請，或透過「聯絡我們」頁面留言時，我們會蒐集您主動提供的姓名、聯絡電話、Email、訂位日期、帳篷數與人數等資訊。若選擇線上刷卡，卡片資訊由綠界科技（ECPay）代為收集與處理，本站及本公司不會接觸或儲存您的完整卡號。 When you submit a booking request or a contact form message, we collect the information you provide: name, phone number, email, booking date, and party size. If you pay by card, your card details are collected and processed directly by ECPay — we never see or store your full card number.

    資料用途How we use it

    您的資料僅用於：確認與管理您的訂位、與您聯繫確認細節、處理付款與退款，以及回覆您透過聯絡表單提出的問題。我們不會將您的個人資料出售或提供給行銷用途之第三方。 Your data is used only to: confirm and manage your booking, contact you about booking details, process payments and refunds, and respond to inquiries. We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

    資料儲存與第三方服務Storage & third-party services

    訂位資料儲存於 Supabase（資料庫代管服務）。線上刷卡交易由綠界科技（ECPay）處理，其隱私權政策請參見綠界科技官網。我們僅授權必要人員（營區管理者）存取訂位資料，用於營運目的。 Booking data is stored with Supabase (database hosting). Card payments are processed by ECPay — see ECPay's own privacy policy for details on their handling of payment data. Access to booking data is limited to authorized campground staff for operational purposes.

    您的權利Your rights

    您可以隨時透過「聯絡我們」頁面或電話 0939-235-168 與我們聯繫，要求查詢、更正或刪除我們持有的您的個人資料。 You may contact us anytime via the Contact page or by phone at 0939-235-168 to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data.
